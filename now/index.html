Thinking About

The pushback against AI isn't about the technology itself — AI has been around for over 50 years in different forms. What people are worried about, myself included, is how it gets used: to disempower people, concentrate power, extract value without returning it. AlphaFold is an example of what's possible when the technology serves people. Flock cameras are the other direction. What we build with it matters more than the technology itself.

Also thinking about personal accountability. Jenny and I have been talking about how much direct influence we have over our own outcomes. I believe you hold yourself 100% accountable for your inputs and let the outputs be what they are. The moment you start blaming your environment, you give up your power to change it.

And underneath both is one of my favorite topics: attention. We have a limited capacity to care deeply, and society fills that capacity constantly. I've become comfortable being apathetic toward most things as a deliberate choice about what's worth the energy. You can't care about everything. You shouldn't feel guilty for not trying.
